Two inks

Sublevel stays monochrome. This colophon uses parchment, warm ink, hairline rules, and one burnt-sienna accent.

Design • Renaissance print shop meets terminal

A margin you can read.

Display type is EB Garamond. Commands and labels are IBM Plex Mono, uppercase and letter-spaced, with the bullet as a separator. Corners are square and rules are hairlines; the only shadow is a one-plate letterpress offset.

The engraved figure is an asset from the imported skill. It fades toward the paper so the headline sits on clean parchment. A fixed double rule frames the whole sheet like a printed plate.
